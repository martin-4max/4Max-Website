IBM Certified Maximo Manage v9.0 Functional Deployment — Professional

Exam C1000-183  ·  Professional level

An IBM Certified Functional Deployment Professional is a technical professional responsible for the functional design and implementation of an IBM Maximo Application Suite — Manage v9.0 deployment. The exam covers nine weighted domains, from the Maximo Application Suite itself through platform configuration, work management and work safety.

IBM Certified Maximo Manage v9.1 Inventory Management — Associate

Exam C1000-208  ·  Associate level

An IBM Maximo Manage v9.1 Inventory Management user is responsible for the daily creation, management, and processing of stock definition and material management within IBM Maximo, primarily using the Inventory, Storeroom, Items and Receiving applications. Associate-level certifications validate foundational skills and assume at least six months of hands-on experience.
