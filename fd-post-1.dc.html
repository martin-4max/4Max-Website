Welcome to the Maximo Mentor Series — your ultimate roadmap to the IBM Maximo Manage v9.0 Functional Deployment certification exam (C1000-183). This series is structured around the nine core exam sections that collectively define the skills needed to pass the certification and work effectively with Maximo.

The Nine Exam Domains

  1. Maximo Application Suite (10% Weight) – Foundational MAS deployment on Red Hat OpenShift, including the Health and Predict applications, plus user administration via identity providers.
  2. Financial Configuration (5% Weight) – Chart of Accounts, GL Components, Budget Monitoring, and Asset Depreciation methods.
  3. Platform Configuration (26% Weight) – Organizations, Sites, Security Groups, Database Configuration, Automation Scripting, and Integration Framework.
  4. Asset & Inventory Management (15% Weight) – Equipment lifecycles, Asset Hierarchies, Meter types, Failure Codes, and inventory control processes.
  5. Procurement & Contract Management (7% Weight) – Requisition-to-Receipt workflows, PR/PO lifecycles, and specialized contracts.
  6. Work Management & Planning (16% Weight) – Job Plans, condition monitoring, Preventive Maintenance, and Work Order Intelligence.
  7. Configure Maximo Scheduler (11% Weight) – Graphical Scheduling, Assignment tools, and optimization models.
  8. Configure Service Management (6% Weight) – Service Request lifecycle and Service Level Agreements.
  9. Configure Work Safety (4% Weight) – Safety Plans, NFPA ratings, and Lockout/Tagout procedures.

Over the coming sessions we'll work through each of these domains in turn, pairing the official exam objectives with practical, real-world configuration tips so you walk away with certification-ready knowledge and genuinely useful Maximo skills. Stay tuned for Session 1, where we start with a high-level introduction to the Maximo Application Suite.
